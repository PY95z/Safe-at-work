Situations

For each situation: the risks, and how to protect yourself.

1. Working in an office

Risks

  • Falling because of cables or open drawers
  • Bad posture on the chair
  • Sitting too long without moving

How to protect yourself

  • Keep the floor clear and close the drawers
  • Adjust the chair so your feet are flat on the floor
  • Stand up and walk a little every hour

2. In front of a computer

Risks

  • Tired and dry eyes
  • Neck, back and wrist pain
  • Headaches

How to protect yourself

  • Rule 20-20-20: every 20 min, look 20 feet away for 20 sec
  • Put the screen at eye level, about one arm away
  • Take a real break every 2 hours

3. In a factory (dangerous machines)

Risks

  • Hands or clothes caught in a machine
  • Cuts, burns and electric shocks
  • Loud noise

How to protect yourself

  • Wear gloves, glasses, helmet and ear protection(PPE)
  • Never remove the safety guards of a machine
  • Turn off and lock the machine before cleaning or repairing
  • No loose clothes, no jewelry, tie your hair

4. Psychological risks

Risks

  • Stress and too much work
  • Burnout (total exhaustion)
  • Harassment or isolation

How to protect yourself

  • Talk to your manager if the workload is too high
  • Respect your break and your free time
  • Speak to a colleague, a union or occupational health
  • Harassment is illegal: keep proof and ask for help

5. Carrying heavy loads

Risks

  • Back injuries
  • Dropping the load on your feet

How to protect yourself

  • Bend your knees, keep your back straight
  • Keep the load close to your body
  • Use a trolley or ask a colleague if it is too heavy

6. Fire and emergency

Risks

  • Fire, smoke and panic
  • Blocked emergency exits

How to protect yourself

  • Know where the exits and the extinguishers are
  • Never block an exit
  • If there is a fire: raise the alarm, leave, do not use the lift
