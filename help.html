Help

How to use this website in 3 steps.

Step 1 - Choose your situation

On the home page, click on the card that looks like your job (office, computer, factory...).

Step 2 - Read the risks

You arrive on the Situations page. Each situation shows the dangers you can meet at work.

Step 3 - Apply the advice

Under the risks, you can find simple actions to protect yourself. You can go back at any time with the menu at the top.

Questions

My situation is not on the website?
Ask your manager or the occupational health service of your company.

Is it an emergency?
Call 112 (Europe) or 999 (UK).
